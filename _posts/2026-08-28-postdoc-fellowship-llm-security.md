### Position

* Postdoc Fellowship
* Funded by [Digital Futures](https://www.digitalfutures.kth.se/) — **2.4M SEK**, 2-year salary
* Hosted at KTH Royal Institute of Technology (Royal Hacking Lab)
* Focus: Developing **LLM agents** to discover vulnerability and/or exploit vulnerabilities in software (web applications) or networks and/or discovering vulnerabilities in **agentic AI attack surfaces**.

### Who should apply

* PhD obtained in **2026**, relevant area (security, ML/AI, systems, or adjacent)
* Interests ideally overlapping LLM security, threat intelligence, adversarial ML, or IoT/embedded security
**Residence:** EU residence permit valid at least until **January 2027**. Extensions possible; **new** permits cannot be issued for this call.
* **Mobility** required: moving to KTH from another institution
* Not open to researchers already based at KTH
* We shape the proposal together; I prepare the support letter
